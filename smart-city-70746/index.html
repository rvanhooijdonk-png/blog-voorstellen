Where these smart cities stand in 2026

Several of the projects above have moved on since this article first appeared. This is where they stand in October 2026, according to the official websites of the cities, governments and companies behind them.

Singapore introduced a refreshed vision, Smart Nation 2.0, in 2024. The government’s Smart Nation website lists the city-state ninth in the 2026 IMD Smart City Index and first in IMD’s 2026 World Digital Competitiveness Ranking.

In Xiong’an, blue and green space has been kept stable at 70 per cent of the area since the New Area was established, according to its official website (in Chinese). In October 2026, the New Area hosted the Global Governance Forum, with guests from more than 90 countries and international organisations.

Toyota Woven City marked the first anniversary of its launch in September 2026. About 160 people in roughly 80 households, including people from surrounding towns, were taking part in some 30 trials, and 31 companies had joined as 'Inventors'. Construction of phase 2 had begun, and Toyota announced that visitors from across Japan would be welcomed to phase 1 from 1 October, according to Woven by Toyota.

In Kalasatama, more than 1,300 new residents moved in during 2025, when about 730 new homes were completed and an estimated 1,000 new jobs were created, according to the City of Helsinki (in Finnish). Finland’s first office skyscraper, the 111-metre Kalasatama Horisontti, was completed that spring.

In October 2025, Neom’s own newsroom reported that DataVolt had signed an agreement to design and develop a net-zero AI factory campus in Oxagon.

Helsingborg’s H22 website now serves as an archive of the city’s 2019-2022 innovation initiative and City Expo.

Hong Kong’s 2026 Policy Address says the government will ‘expedite the development of smart government, digital infrastructure and application scenarios’.

We could not find a recent update from the developers of the Smart Forest City in Cancun, Net City, Chengdu Future City or Sandbox City.
